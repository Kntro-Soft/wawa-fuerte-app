# Authors — Wawa Fuerte

This app is built by the **Kntro-Soft** team for the **Build with Gemma: GDG Callao** hackathon
(Lima, Peru — 25 July 2026), a one-day sprint aligned with the United Nations Sustainable
Development Goals **SDG 2 (Zero Hunger)** and **SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being)**.

It is an offline-first Flutter application that runs Gemma entirely on-device to generate weekly
nutrition plans against childhood anemia, grounded in the official recipe book of Peru's
Instituto Nacional de Salud.
